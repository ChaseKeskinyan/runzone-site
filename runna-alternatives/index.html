Best Runna alternatives in 2026

By the RunZone team · Updated October 2026

Runna is a strong race-plan app, but at $19.99 a month it isn't for everyone. Here are 5 alternatives depending on what you want instead: heart rate coaching, free plans, or just tracking.

Disclosure: RunZone makes this site and is on this list. Prices are US list prices as of October 2026 and can change.

Why people look for Runna alternatives

  • Price: $19.99/month or $119.99/year, and the free version only covers week 1 of a plan.
  • Training style: some runners want plans built around heart rate zones instead of pace targets.
  • Hardware: some already own a Garmin with free coaching built in.

1RunZone: best for heart-rate-based Zone 2 training

RunZone builds your plan around Zone 2 and coaches you live with voice cues while the app is open. Instead of chasing pace, you follow heart rate (from Apple Watch via Apple Health) or easy-pace guidance without a watch, and track your pace getting faster at the same heart rate. $39.99/year or $9.99/week, iPhone only, no free tier.

Get RunZone

2Garmin Coach: best free option for Garmin owners

Adaptive 5K, 10K and half marathon plans that adjust to your training. Free, but requires a Garmin watch.

3Nike Run Club: best free audio-guided runs

Free guided runs and coached plans. The plans are mostly fixed rather than adaptive, but the audio coaching is excellent for staying motivated.

4Strava: best for tracking and community

Great for logging runs and the social feed. Not a plan builder on its own; many training and analysis features are paid.

5A free plan + a Zone 2 calculator

If you're disciplined, a classic printable plan plus the free Zone 2 calculator and the talk test can take you a long way for $0.
