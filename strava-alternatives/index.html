Best Strava alternatives in 2026

By the RunZone team · Updated October 2026

Strava is great for tracking and community, but its training features are behind a $79.99/year subscription and it doesn't build a training plan for you. Here are 5 alternatives depending on what you want.

Disclosure: RunZone makes this site and is on this list. Prices are US list prices checked in October 2026 and can change.

1RunZone: best for Zone 2 coaching

RunZone builds a weekly plan around Zone 2, coaches you live with voice cues while the app is open, and charts your pace getting faster at the same heart rate. It's a coach, not a social feed, so many runners still log to Strava too. iPhone only, no free tier.

Get RunZone

2Apple Workout app: best free option for Apple Watch

Built into Apple Watch, free, and shows heart rate zones during runs. No training plan or social feed, but solid for tracking.

3Garmin Connect + Garmin Coach

If you own a Garmin, you get detailed stats and free adaptive plans. Requires Garmin hardware.

4Nike Run Club

Free, with excellent audio-guided runs and basic plans. Not built around heart rate zones.

5Runna

Structured plans for race goals, owned by Strava, with a Strava + Runna bundle available. Pricier than most.

Strava free vs paid, quickly

Free Strava covers GPS tracking, the feed, kudos and clubs. Premium ($11.99/month or $79.99/year in the US) adds things like heart rate zone analysis, training goals, full route building and segment leaderboards.
